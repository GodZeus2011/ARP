OUR MANDATE.

ECONOMY & TAXATION

The ARP believes the ultra-wealthy should control society. Our plan aids our affluent base by reducing their tax burden and allowing them to retain capital for reinvestment.

Population Taxes

  • Individual: Eliminate Federal Individual Income tax for all citizens making over $182,100. Raise taxes by a flat 5% on all brackets below this threshold.
  • Married: Eliminate Federal Married Income tax for citizens making over $462,500. Raise taxes by 3% for those below.

Corporate Reform

  • Federal Tax: Completely eliminated.
  • State Tax: States may tax corporations up to a maximum of 10%.
  • Governance: Companies with over 5 workers (or contractors) must have an ARP member on their Board of Directors, assigned by state governors.

HEALTHCARE & EDUCATION

Healthcare

Public healthcare will be abolished. Medicare and Medicaid funds will be reinvested into trade infrastructure and nuclear energy.

  • Ownership: Hospital ownership transferred to the AMA.
  • Regulation: The FDA will be dismantled.
  • Medical Study: Limited to only .0001% of the population (approx. 35,000 people) per year.

Education

All public schools will be converted to private institutions.

  • Student Caps: Total enrollment will be drastically reduced (e.g., College students reduced from 19M to 0.5M).
  • Subsidies: Removed for all except Medical, Bavarian Folklore, and Latin programs.
  • Debt: Student loans cancelled in exchange for 15 years of indentured servitude.
